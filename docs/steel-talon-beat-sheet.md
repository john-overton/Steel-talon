# STEEL TALON: OPERATION GREENFIRE
### Gnarcade Launch Title, 10-Level Beat Sheet (working draft)

Working title is a placeholder. Tone target: late 80s action movie by way of a Saturday morning cartoon. Top Gun, Predator, and Airwolf in a blender. Every line should be deliverable with a wink.

---

## Cast

**Lt. Cmdr. Jake "Tex" Malone** - The player. Best stick in the fleet, allergic to authority, never misses. Face appears only in cutscene portraits: aviators, jawline, toothpick.

**Lt. Dana "Vixen" Cruz** - Flight intelligence officer. Delivers every briefing. Smart, deadpan, visibly unimpressed by Tex, obviously the romance arc. Her eye-roll is a recurring cutscene gag.

**Admiral "Bulldog" Hayes** - Commander, USS Wasp. Talks entirely in gravel. Gets one line per level, usually over the intercom, usually iconic.

**Preston Vayne** - The villain. Ex-defense contractor CEO of Cerberus Applied Dynamics. White linen suit, silver ponytail, jungle fortress. Stole his own company's autonomous swarm AI (codename MEDUSA) after the Pentagon canceled the program, and is now "demonstrating the product" by conquering the Amazon basin. Starts broadcasting smug pirate-TV monologues to the fleet at Level 4.

**"Anaconda"** - Vayne's personal bodyguard pilot and the only human aviator on the enemy side. Flies a jet-black prototype gunship. Rival boss, fought twice.

---

## Structure Notes

- 10 levels, 2 to 5 minutes each. Level 2 is the long showcase at 6 to 8 minutes.
- Three modes: **TOP** (vertical scrolling shmup), **SIDE** (Contra-style side scroll), **ROAM** (Desert Strike free-roam arena). All three taught in Level 1.
- Roguelike layer: death ends the run. Salvage collected in-level buys permanent chopper upgrades (armor, weapons, wingman drone, extra life) between runs at the hangar screen. Level themes are fixed, enemy waves inside them are procedurally mixed.
- Cutscenes are 2 to 4 static illustrated panels with text, skippable, 15 to 30 seconds. Briefings are a Vixen portrait plus a tactical map with pushpins and red yarn.

## LEVEL 1 - "GOOD SHOOTING, TEX" (Training)
**Modes: TOP into SIDE into ROAM | 3 min | Caribbean, off the USS Wasp**

Live-fire certification drill. Sunny water, steel drums under the chiptune.

- **TOP:** Fly the outbound lane shooting drone boats and target aircraft. Teaches movement, primary gun, missile pickup.
- **SIDE:** Course turns into a target gulch cut into an island: pop-up plywood tanks, balloon targets, cave mouth. Teaches side-scroll gravity, altitude, and rear firing.
- **ROAM:** Open bay drill arena. Boss: a big orange training quadcopter firing dummy missiles and low-watt lasers. Teaches strafing, circling, and lock-ons.
- **The hook:** At roughly 3/4 health the quadcopter powers down mid-fight, mid-attack. Intercom crackles: *"Good shooting, Tex. Get back to the barn. We've got a situation developing."*

**Cutscene:** Ready room. Vixen at the map. *"Unknown forces are hitting government targets across South America, expanding out of the deep southern Amazon. Nobody claims them. Nobody recognizes the airframes. The Wasp is being redirected to interdict. Wheels up in six hours, hotshot. Try to stay awake for the briefing next time."*

---

## LEVEL 2 - "BEACHHEAD" (The Showcase)
**Modes: TOP into SIDE into ROAM | 6-8 min | Southern coast of Brazil**

You lift off from the Wasp into a fleet actively under attack. Harriers and destroyers are trading fire with unidentified delta-wing aircraft and go-fast attack boats.

- **Briefing (in-cockpit, Vixen inset at bottom of screen):** *"Fight your way to the coastline and open up the coastal defenses. Evade or kill everything between you and the beach. Once you're feet dry, clear the emplacements so the Marines have somewhere to land."*
- **TOP:** Running battle through the fleet fight. Delta-wings, missile volleys, surface fire from fast boats. Friendly ships shoot too, so the screen feels like a war, not a shooting gallery.
- **SIDE:** At the coast the view slams to side-scroll. Beach and mountains. Shallow-water gunboats, bunkers, AA nests, flak guns, pillboxes, sandbag emplacements, all of it destructible.
- **ROAM Boss:** Past the headland, a hidden bay: an automated **dreadnought** bristling with missile racks and AA. Full free-roam fight, circling the bay, dodging arcing fire, killing turret sections in any order until the magazine cooks off.

**Cutscene:** Marines wade ashore at sunset. Tex low-passes the beach, a Marine salutes with a cigar. Vixen over radio: *"Show-off."* First hard cut to the jungle: a monitor wall, a white suit, a hand swirling a drink. We do not see his face yet.

---

## LEVEL 3 - "GREEN HELL RUN"
**Mode: SIDE | 3-4 min | Lower Amazon river**

Escort mission upriver. Landing craft below you, canyon walls and triple canopy around you.

- Side-scroll over the river: gun barges, rope-bridge infantry, mortar positions on the banks, delta-wings making strafing runs down the river corridor.
- The landing craft can take hits, and losing them costs salvage bonus, not the run. Protect greed, not survival.
- **Mini-boss:** A chain ferry pulled across the river as a mobile flak wall.

**Cutscene:** Recovered wreckage on the Wasp's deck. Vixen holds up a scorched circuit board. *"No pilots, Tex. There were never any pilots. These things are flying themselves."* Bulldog, chewing a cigar: *"Then whoever built 'em is my new favorite target."*

---

## LEVEL 4 - "NIGHT MOVES"
**Mode: TOP | 3-4 min | Coastal jungle, night**

Night palette, searchlights, radar dishes. Destroy the comms and radar arrays guiding the swarm's coastal net.

- Searchlight cones sweep the screen. Getting lit calls a hornet wave of interceptors. Not stealth exactly, but light-avoidance as a shmup mechanic.
- Radar dishes are the objectives; everything else is optional salvage.
- **The reveal:** The last dish explodes and every screen on the HUD glitches to a pirate broadcast. Silver ponytail, white suit, tumbler of something amber. **Preston Vayne:** *"Impressive shooting, Commander. I should know. I wrote the software you're outflying. Cerberus Applied Dynamics thanks you for the live product demonstration."*

**Cutscene:** Vixen slams a dossier on the table. *"Preston Vayne. The Pentagon canceled his autonomous swarm program two years ago. Ethics review. He didn't take it well."* Photo of a jungle caldera fortress. *"He took the inventory."*

---

## LEVEL 5 - "FRIENDLY FIRE"
**Modes: TOP into ROAM | 4 min | Over the fleet, dawn**

The payoff of the training level. MEDUSA hijacks the fleet's own drone escorts mid-patrol. The quadcopter that "shut down" in Level 1 was Vayne probing the back door.

- **TOP:** Your own wingman drones peel off formation and open fire. Fighting yesterday's allies: same sprites, new red glow. Genuine gut-punch moment played completely straight by the cast.
- **ROAM Boss:** The Wasp's prototype heavy escort drone, now hostile, over the open sea. It uses YOUR upgrade weapons against you, whatever the player has purchased so far, mirrored.
- **Vayne mid-level broadcast:** *"Don't blame the machines, Commander. Loyalty was never in the spec."*

**Cutscene:** Bulldog orders every remaining drone craned overboard. *"If it thinks for itself, it swims."* Vixen, quietly, to Tex: *"That leaves you."* The eye-roll is replaced, one time only, by something almost warm.

---

## LEVEL 6 - "SNAKE EATER" (The Stealth Strike)
**Modes: ROAM (stealth) into SIDE | 4-5 min | Deep jungle, behind the SAM line**

The Desert Strike homage. A lone deep strike on the jamming station that blinds allied air cover.

- **ROAM:** Free-roam ingress across a SAM-saturated grid. Radar circles on the minimap, terrain masking behind ridgelines, strict fuel timer, one target. Guns-cold is optional but every kill wakes the grid up further.
- Strike the jammer. Sirens. Everything lights up at once.
- **SIDE (RTB):** The escape leg is a full-throttle side-scroll sprint back to the coast with the entire grid awake: interceptor waves behind you, SAMs arcing in from the background layer, low fuel. Pure adrenaline exhale after the tension inhale.

**Cutscene:** Tex lands on fumes, engine literally coughing. Deck crew cheers. Vixen hands him a coffee. *"Jamming's down. Air wing's up. You look terrible."* Beat. *"Good work."*

---

## LEVEL 7 - "ANACONDA"
**Modes: SIDE into ROAM | 4 min | Canyon country at the Amazon headwaters**

The rival fight. Vayne sends his one human asset.

- **SIDE:** High-speed canyon chase, Anaconda's black gunship dueling you through rock arches, waterfalls, and collapsing cliff walls, dropping mines in your flight path.
- **ROAM Boss:** The canyon opens into a crater lake arena. Proper ace duel: he strafes, feints, and baits like a player, not a pattern. First human voice on the enemy net: *"Vayne pays me by the kill, Navy. You're worth a bonus."*
- He escapes at 20% health, trailing smoke. *"Next time, cowboy."*

**Cutscene:** Vixen: *"Sat photos put the fortress inside an extinct caldera, here. One way in through the dam gorge. It's a fortress, Tex."* Tex flips his aviators down. *"So knock."*

---

## LEVEL 8 - "HOLD THE LINE"
**Mode: TOP | 3-4 min | Over the Wasp battle group**

Vayne empties the arsenal at the fleet before you can reach him. Pure wave defense, the loudest level in the game.

- Massed swarm attack from all vectors: kamikaze drones, torpedo boats, high-altitude bombers. The Wasp has a visible health bar for the first time.
- Escalating waves with a salvage bonus per intact friendly ship. Screen should approach bullet-hell density by the final wave.
- **Climax:** A cruise missile barrage from over the horizon that must be shot down, Missile Command style, as the finale.

**Cutscene:** Fires on the flight deck, foam everywhere. Bulldog on the 1MC, silhouetted against smoke: *"He threw his best punch. We're still floating. Commander Malone: go ruin that man's morning."*

---

## LEVEL 9 - "THE DAM"
**Mode: SIDE | 4-5 min | The gorge approach to the caldera**

The gauntlet. Hardest side-scroll in the game, straight up the fortress's front porch.

- Vertical-feeling ascent staged in tiers up the dam and gorge wall: gun galleries, flak towers, laser fences that cycle, hangar mouths spitting interceptors, cargo cranes swinging as moving cover.
- Mid-level checkpoint at a captured pumphouse, the only mid-level checkpoint in the game, because this level is mean.
- **Mini-boss:** The dam's crest: twin rail turrets that must be destroyed near-simultaneously, blowing the floodgates and draining Vayne's cooling reservoir. His fortress starts overheating. MEDUSA gets glitchier from here on.

**Cutscene:** Water thunders into the valley. Alarms inside the fortress, red light on white linen. Vayne, calm, finishing his drink: *"Warm up my ship."*

---

## LEVEL 10 - "HEART OF THE MACHINE"
**Modes: ROAM into SIDE | 5 min | Inside the caldera**

Everything, all at once.

- **ROAM Phase 1:** The caldera interior is the final arena. Destroy MEDUSA's four uplink towers while the overheating AI throws corrupted, misfiring swarm patterns at you. Anaconda returns mid-phase, glitching drones attacking him too. Optional beat: ignore him and MEDUSA kills him for you; kill him yourself for bonus salvage and his taunt, *"...good hunting, cowboy."*
- **ROAM Phase 2:** Vayne rises from the silo in the **Cerberus One**, a three-headed command gunship: rotary lasers, missile pods, and a MEDUSA-linked shield that only drops when his monologue does. He literally cannot resist talking; interrupting his broadcast antenna is the mechanic.
- **SIDE Finale:** Core goes critical. Full-throttle side-scroll escape out through the collapsing dam gorge, fireball chasing, debris falling, one last sprint through the level 9 wreckage in reverse.

**Ending cutscene:** The caldera erupts behind the silhouette of one battered chopper. Beach at sunset, the Marines from Level 2, Bulldog with two cigars, handing one to Tex. Vixen takes off Tex's aviators and puts them on herself. Freeze frame mid high-five. Title card: **"TEX WILL RETURN."** Post-credits stinger: deep ocean floor, a single MEDUSA node blinks back on.

| 2 | T/S/R | 6-8 | Full sandwich showcase |
| 3 | S | 3-4 | Escort greed |
| 4 | T | 3-4 | Searchlight avoidance |
| 5 | T/R | 4 | Mirror-match boss |
| 6 | R/S | 4-5 | Stealth tension, fuel timer |
| 7 | S/R | 4 | Human ace AI |
| 8 | T | 3-4 | Bullet-hell density |
| 9 | S | 4-5 | Endurance gauntlet |
| 10 | R/S | 5 | Everything |

Modes: T = top-down, S = side scroll, R = free roam.
